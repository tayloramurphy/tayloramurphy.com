# Track outlines

Background circuits are real racing lines, simplified for the canvas.

- Formula 1 layouts from [bacinger/f1-circuits](https://github.com/bacinger/f1-circuits) (MIT). Unofficial; not associated with Formula One companies.
- Additional ovals and endurance circuits from OpenStreetMap (`highway=raceway`), © OpenStreetMap contributors, [ODbL](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/).
